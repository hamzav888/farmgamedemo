Sunny Barn Farm

A gentle first farm game. Tap the animals to hear their real sounds and learn their names, watch the day turn to night, and count the sleeping animals in the big red barn. A friendly narrator does all the reading.

What's on the farm

Tap, listen, count

Everything a one-year-old can do is a tap. Everything a four-year-old wants is in there too.

🐄

Tap an animal

Cow! This is a cow. The cow says Moo! A big name card, a real recording of the animal, and a trick on the second tap: the pig rolls in the mud, the chicken lays an egg, the duck dives.

🌙

Day turns to night

At dusk the animals walk into the barn and the doors close. Fireflies come out, an owl hoots in the tree, frogs croak on the lily pads, and a music-box lullaby takes over from the banjo.

🧮

Count them to sleep

Inside the barn the animals snore in little rows. Tap each one and the narrator counts with you: One… two… three! There are three pigs! The quiz then zooms in on just the cats, so there is only one thing to count.

⭐

Collect stars

Every count, quiz answer and listening round earns a star, and they are kept between visits. Count every animal in the barn for a three-star firework show.

After dark

The farm goes to bed

Night comes by itself every couple of minutes, or at the tap of the 🌞 button. It is the calmest part of the game, and the part where the counting happens.

Mini-games

Seven little games, one farm

Each one teaches a different thing, drawn in the farm's own art and spoken by the farm's own narrator. Tap 🎮 on the farm to pick one. Stars earned here count on the farm too.

🎵 Farm Band

Five animals on coloured drums. Every tap is a banjo note, so anything bashed out sounds like a tune. Copy the rooster's tune for a concert.

Cause & effect · memory

⭐ Star Animals

Night-sky dot-to-dot. Join the stars one, two, three and the lines light up; finish, and a real animal fades into the constellation to say hello.

Number order 1–7

🚜 Tractor Ride

Tap anywhere and the tractor drives there, trailer bouncing behind. Scoop up the hay bales, splash through puddles, honk at the dog, then deliver the load to the barn.

Cause & effect · counting

🥕 Veggie Patch

Drag vegetables from the crate into the shaped holes in the scarecrow's garden. Round three puts carrot and pumpkin, both orange, together, so the shape has to do the work.

Shapes · colour words

🔍 Hide & Seek

Where's the cow? Behind the tree, in the hay with her ears showing, or in the pond with her eyes above the water. Stuck? After a moment she moos.

Observation · listening

🐷 Runaway Piglets

The piglets have escaped! Tap one to catch it and it floats back to the sty. They get faster each round, and the dog's bark freezes them for a moment.

Tracking · reflexes

🛁 Muddy Pig Bath

Rub anywhere and the sponge wipes the mud off a huge pig, with bubbles and giggles. Clean, he jumps for joy, then rolls in the mud again.

Motor control · clean & dirty
Sound

Real animals, a real voice

Every animal is a real recording, and the narrator has a warm, natural voice that names, counts and cheers. By day there is a banjo tune and birdsong; by night, crickets, an owl and a lullaby. Turn the sound on 🔊 before the first tap and let the farm do the talking.

For parents

Made for little hands

Built for a one-year-old on a parent's phone and a four-year-old on the family tablet, and nothing in between to trip over.

What you can count on

  • Nothing to read. The narrator runs everything, so it works before letters do.
  • No fail states. A wrong answer is a gentle let's count them together, then another go.
  • Big targets. Every animal has an extra-large touch area, and the buttons grow on small screens.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, nothing collected. Stars are saved on the device only.
  • Plays offline after the first visit, so it works in the car.
  • Best in landscape on a phone or tablet. It works on a desktop too.

Put it on the home screen

  1. iPhone or iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open it from the icon and it fills the screen like any other app, sound and all.

Tip: the first tap on ▶ Let's Play! is what lets the sound start, so turn the volume up before you hand it over.
