🐄 Sunny Barn Farm 🐔

A farm to explore, listen to, and count on! Ages 1–5

  • 👆
    Tap an animal to hear its name and sound
  • ✌️
    Tap it twice to see it do a trick
  • 🏠
    Tap the big red barn to go inside
  • 🌞🌙
    Use the sun / moon button to change day and night
  • 🔢
    At night, count the sleeping animals in the barn!
  • 🎮
    Seven mini-games, one friendly narrator

Scroll down for a sneak peek 👇

📱 Phone, tablet or desktop
👆

Tap, listen, count!

Everything a one-year-old can do is a tap. Everything a four-year-old wants is in there too.

Tap an animal

A big name card, the animal's real call, and a trick on the second tap: the pig rolls in the mud, the chicken lays an egg, the duck dives.

“Cow! This is a cow. The cow says Moo!”

Day turns to night

At dusk the animals walk into the barn and the doors close. Fireflies come out, an owl hoots in the tree, frogs croak on the lily pads, and a music-box lullaby takes over from the banjo.

z z

Count them to sleep

Inside the barn the animals snore in little rows. Tap each one and the narrator counts with you: one… two… three! Then a quiz with just the pigs to count.

Collect stars

Every count, quiz answer and listening round earns a star, kept between visits. Count every animal in the barn for a three-star firework show.

⭐ 12
🌙

The farm goes to bed

Night comes by itself every couple of minutes, or at a tap of the 🌞 button. It is the calmest part of the game, and where the counting happens.

z z zz z
How many pigs are sleeping?

🔊

Real animals, a real voice

Every animal is a real recording, and the narrator has a warm voice that names, counts and cheers. Banjo and birdsong by day; crickets, an owl and a lullaby by night.

Six lines from the narrator, 30 seconds.

👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

Made for little hands

  • 💚
    No fail states. A wrong answer is a gentle “let's count them together”, then another go.
  • 🖐️
    Big targets. Every animal has an extra-large touch area, and the buttons grow on small screens.
  • 🔒
    No accounts, nothing collected. Stars are saved on the device only.
  • 📱
    Best in landscape on a phone or tablet. Works on a desktop too.
