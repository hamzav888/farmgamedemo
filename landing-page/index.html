What’s inside

Everything a one-year-old can do is a tap.

And everything a four-year-old wants is in there too: names, sounds, counting, a quiz and a barn full of sleepy animals.

Tap an animal

A big name card, the animal’s real call, and a trick on the second tap: the pig rolls in the mud, the chicken lays an egg, the duck dives.

Day turns to night

At dusk the animals walk into the barn and the doors close. Fireflies come out, an owl hoots in the tree and a music-box lullaby takes over from the banjo.

Count them to sleep

Inside the barn the animals snore in little rows. Tap each one and the narrator counts with you: one… two… three! Then a quiz with just the pigs to count.

Collect stars

Every count, quiz answer and listening round earns a star, kept between visits. Count every animal in the barn for a three-star firework show.

Night time

The calmest part of the game.

Night comes by itself every couple of minutes, or at a tap of the sun button. It is where the counting happens.

  1. 1
    The animals head inside. They walk into the barn one by one and the doors close behind them.
  2. 2
    Tap the barn to go in. The animals snore in little rows, each in its own stall.
  3. 3
    Count along. Tap each sleeping animal and the narrator counts with you, then asks how many.

How many pigs are sleeping?

Try the barn quiz. A wrong answer gets a gentle count-along, never a buzzer.

For parents

Made for little hands.

Designed around how one-to-five-year-olds actually play: big targets, no pressure, and nothing to set up.

How it’s built

The small decisions that make it calm.

  • No fail states. A wrong answer is a gentle “let’s count them together”, then another go.
  • Big targets. Every animal has an extra-large touch area, and the buttons grow on small screens.
  • No accounts, nothing collected. Stars are saved on the device only.
  • Best in landscape on a phone or tablet. Works on a desktop too.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When does it launch?

Soon. The date will be announced on this page first, so bookmark it or pass it along to a friend.

Which devices will it work on?

Phones, tablets and desktop. It opens in the browser with nothing to install and can sit on the home screen like any other app. It plays best in landscape.

What ages is it for?

One to five. A one-year-old can tap the animals and hear them; a four-year-old gets the counting, the quiz and the mini-games.

Does it collect any data?

No. There are no accounts and nothing is collected. Stars are saved on the device only.

What will be in it at launch?

The whole farm: the animals, day and night, the barn count and all seven mini-games.
